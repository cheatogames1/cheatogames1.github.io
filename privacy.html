Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Deep Pearl Tycoon (Android, package name com.CheatoGames.DeepPearlTycoon). It explains which data is processed while you play, why, and with whom it is shared.

1. Contact

Cheato Games
Email: cheato.games1@gmail.com

2. No account, no own servers

You do not need an account to play. We do not run our own servers and do not collect names, email addresses, locations or contacts ourselves. We do not sell any data.

3. Save data on your device

Your game progress is stored encrypted on your device only. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Cloud save via Google Play Games (optional)

If you connect “Google Play” in the settings, your save game is stored in your Google Play Games account so you can restore it on another device. Google processes your player ID and save data for this. Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (feature requested by you). You can unlink at any time in the settings and delete stored data in your Google account or the Play Games app.
Google privacy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Advertising

The game only shows optional rewarded ads (e.g. for a boost). We use the ad platform Unity LevelPlay with the ad networks Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These providers may process: advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language setting, approximate region and information about which ads were shown and watched. Purpose: delivering and billing ads, fraud prevention and – only with your consent – personalised ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent on first launch using Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game settings under “Privacy”. Without consent, at most non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings. After buying “No Ads” no ads are shown.

Providers:
· Unity Technologies (LevelPlay, Unity Ads) – unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
· Google Ireland Limited (AdMob) – policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU (in particular the USA). The providers use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for this.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases (diamonds, No Ads) are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive any payment details, only a purchase receipt (product, order number) that is checked for authenticity inside the game. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of contract).

7. Internet connection, time and updates

The game requires an internet connection. To prevent manipulation of the device clock, it reads the current time from public web servers on launch (google.com, cloudflare.com, microsoft.com). As with any website request, your IP address is transmitted to these servers; no other data is sent. The game also checks via Google Play whether an update is available. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in fair play and an up-to-date version).

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. As we do not store personal data ourselves, please contact the providers listed above directly regarding data they hold; we are happy to help at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the game or the law changes. The version published here applies.
